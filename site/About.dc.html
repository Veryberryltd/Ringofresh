About Ringo Fresh

Built as a wholesale operation, from import to delivery.

Ringo Fresh runs as a dedicated wholesale division for fruits and vegetables across Tanzania and Zanzibar. We keep the chain — import, packaging, cold storage and distribution — under one roof so we can hold prices down for customers without cutting corners on freshness.

[ team / warehouse photo ]

Why we exist

Markets, retailers and distributors across Tanzania and Zanzibar need consistent supply at a price that lets them stay competitive. Buying direct from scattered growers doesn't guarantee either. Ringo Fresh was set up to solve both problems at once — as a single wholesale supplier holding volume, quality and price steady.

Every order moves through the same disciplined chain: imported or sourced at scale, sorted and packaged at our pack house, held in cold storage, then shipped or delivered anywhere in Tanzania or Zanzibar.
