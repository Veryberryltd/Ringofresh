Our facilities

Everything produce needs to stay fresh, under one operation.

Import, pack house and cold chain work together as one continuous line — so produce is handled once, packed properly, and never sits outside the cold chain.

01

Direct import lines

We import produce directly at wholesale volume, giving us control over quality and consistent pricing before it ever reaches the pack house.

02

Our own pack house

Every order is sorted, graded and packaged in-house, so what leaves our facility meets the same standard every time — no matter the order size.

03

Continuous cold chain

Our cold chain facility holds produce at the right temperature from pack house to dispatch, so freshness doesn't drop between storage and delivery.

04

Shipping across Tanzania & Zanzibar

From dispatch, orders are shipped or delivered anywhere in Tanzania or Zanzibar — arranged directly with our sales office.
